Features

Everything You Need
to Find Your Phone

A powerful launcher packed with phone-finding tools, anti-theft protection, and a clean home screen experience.

👏

Find by Clap

With detection enabled, clap clearly twice in quick succession to trigger your chosen alert sound. Detection depends on distance and background noise.

🎵

Find by Whistle

Whistle to trigger the alert sound when detection is enabled. Adjust the sensitivity and test detection to suit your surroundings.

🔊

Custom Alert Sounds

Choose your alert sound from Air Horn, Bell, Siren, or Whistle Alert. Pick whatever you'll hear most easily.

🛡️

Anti-Theft Protection

Motion detection triggers an alarm if your phone is moved without permission. Charger alarm alerts you if the charger is unplugged.

🏠

Full Home Screen Launcher

Choose the App as your default launcher for a customizable home screen and app drawer, or keep Pixel Launcher and open the App for phone-finding tools.

⚡

Quick-Toggle Widget

Add the Find Phone widget to your home screen to enable or disable clap & whistle detection with a single tap.

🔋

Battery Efficient

How It Works

Up and Running
in 3 Easy Steps

Getting started takes less than a minute. No account, no sign-up required.

1

Install & Choose Your Home App

Download Find My Phone by Clap Home and complete the setup. Choose this App or keep your preferred default home app, such as Pixel Launcher. After setup, open the App to access phone-finding tools; if you skip the home-app reminder, swipe right from the App's home screen to reach them.

2

Enable Clap & Whistle Detection

Enable detection from the phone-finding screen or the home screen widget. Grant microphone and notification permissions when prompted. Sound recognition runs locally on your device.

3

Clap or Whistle to Find Your Phone

With detection active, clap clearly twice or whistle to trigger your chosen alert sound. Test the sensitivity and volume before relying on the feature.

Privacy & Permissions

Transparent About What We Access

We only request permissions that are strictly necessary. Here's exactly what we use and why.

🎤

Microphone

Used to analyze clap and whistle sounds locally while detection is listening. Listening may continue with the screen on, depending on detection settings and test mode. Audio recordings are not saved or uploaded.

🔔

Notifications

Required to show a persistent status when detection is active, so the service continues running in the background.

🏠

Default Launcher

Optional for replacing your home screen. You can keep Pixel Launcher and use phone-finding tools in the App. A home-app reminder may appear when you leave the phone-finding screen; change your default in Android settings at any time.

📱

Device Sensors

Accelerometer is used only for the optional Anti-Theft motion detection feature. Disabled when anti-theft is off.

🔦

Camera / Flashlight

Camera access supports the optional flashing-light alert. The phone-finding feature does not capture photos or videos.
