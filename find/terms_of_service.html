2. Description of the App

Find My Phone by Clap Home is an Android application that helps users locate their device by clapping or whistling while detection is enabled. It also provides a home screen, app drawer, quick-toggle widget, configurable sound, vibration and flashlight alerts, and optional motion or charger-disconnection anti-theft alerts. Features and availability may vary by device, configuration, and region.

You can set the App as your default home app or keep your existing launcher, including Pixel Launcher. After completing setup, you can access phone-finding tools inside the App. If you skip the home-app reminder, swipe right from the App's home screen to reach those tools. The App may show a default-home-app reminder when you leave the phone-finding screen. Android controls the final default-home selection, which you can change at any time in system settings.

Detection depends on microphone permission, service availability, sensitivity, distance, background noise, and device power-management settings. Test your chosen alerts before relying on them. Phone-finding and anti-theft alerts are convenience features and do not guarantee recovery of a lost or stolen device.
